name: vietnam-job-fit
description: Discover policy-permitted Vietnam vacancies and compare a full JD with approved candidate evidence. Use for job shortlisting and requirement analysis, not for automatic application or invented ATS scores.
---

Read ../../../prompts/MASTER_PROMPT.md and ../../../templates/platform-register.json. Inputs: approved track/location/preferences, evidence ledger and a JD or user authorization to discover a bounded batch.

Check current destination policy before assisted access. Search English/Vietnamese duty variants on permitted sources and prefer employer postings. Preserve raw JD, exact location, work model, IDs, recipient, deadline, UTC retrieval and active/closed/unverified status. A snippet or Apply button alone is insufficient. Unknown policy stays manual; unreadable is unverified.

Deduplicate stable platform/job and nonempty employer requisition IDs. Preserve URL parameters; flag fuzzy duplicates. Normalize city aliases without inferring relocation. Record salary exactly with currency, period, gross/net and commission/bonus gaps. Remote does not prove Vietnam eligibility.

Map mandatory/preferred/uncertain requirements to evidence, direct/partial/missing/unknown match, gap and treatment. Recommend strong fit, plausible fit, stretch or blocked-by-explicit-requirement. Distinguish sales, pre-sales, BA, product and engineering duties.

Output a private shortlist, JD snapshot and evidence matrix. Do not fill forms, add skills from the JD, publish employer text as a dataset or produce interview probabilities.
